The Viking HRTF dataset v2 — reference head and torso responses
Authors: Simone Spagnol, Riccardo Miccini and Rúnar Unnthórsson (2020).
Source and citation: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4160401
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Full legal code: CC-BY-4.0.txt.
Additional requested citation: Simone Spagnol, Kristján Bjarki Purkhús,
Sverrir Karl Björnsson and Rúnar Unnthórsson (2019), The Viking HRTF dataset,
Proceedings of the 16th Sound & Music Computing Conference, pp. 55–60.

The source is the pinna-less subject Z SOFA distributed with the pinned
ITSADIVE repository. The Android reference selects 729 valid directions
on the 10-degree elevation grid and stores them in a compact binary file.
Output profiles modify these reference responses using photo-derived
ear-filter estimates, minimum-phase FIR synthesis, analytical head-size
interaural delays and one common normalization gain. This application and
its output are adaptations, not the original measured VIKING dataset.
