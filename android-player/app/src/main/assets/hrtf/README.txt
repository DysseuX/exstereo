Bundled HRTF measurements

Default: Steam Audio built-in HRTF (part of Steam Audio SDK).
MIT KEMAR: mit_kemar_normal_pinna.sofa
  https://sofacoustics.org/data/database/mit/
SADIE H3: H3_48K_24bit_256tap_FIR_SOFA.sofa, renamed sadie_h3.sofa
SADIE H10: H10_48K_24bit_256tap_FIR_SOFA.sofa, renamed sadie_h10.sofa
  https://sofacoustics.org/data/database/sadie/

The SOFA files are unmodified measurements distributed by the SOFA database.
Their embedded author, license and reference metadata is retained.
Exact source URLs, original sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in sources.json.

These are selectable listener HRTFs, not Sony personalization profiles or
WH-1000XM5 frequency-response correction filters. One HRTF is used at a time.
